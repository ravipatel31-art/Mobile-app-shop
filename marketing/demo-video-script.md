# Cafe POS — 60-second demo video (storyboard)

This is your #1 sales asset. It's written so you can **screen-record the app once,
then assemble it in Canva** in under an hour — no video skills needed.

**The one rule:** show the real app, moving. No slides, no stock footage. The polish
you built (dark + gold UI) *is* the pitch.

---

## 0. Before you record (5 min)

- [ ] Log in with **demo data** — a menu with ~12 items, a few orders already in.
      A blank screen kills the video.
- [ ] Turn **Dark Mode on** in the app (it's locked to dark already) and on the phone.
- [ ] **Clear the status bar**: no WhatsApp banners, no low-battery, full signal, Do Not Disturb ON.
- [ ] Pick a **calm wallpaper** or none — the phone is the stage.
- [ ] **Charge the phone** and close every other app.
- [ ] Decide the numbers you'll show (₹ amounts). Consistency looks professional.

**How to record (iPhone):** Settings → Control Centre → add *Screen Recording* →
swipe down, tap the record button, 3-2-1, then move slowly and deliberately.
**Record each scene separately** (it's far easier to edit than one long take).
Film **portrait (1080×1920)** — best for Google Ads + Reels.

---

## 1. The 60-second storyboard

Record the clips in this order, then drop them into Canva on the timeline.

| # | Time | Screen action (record this) | Text overlay in Canva | VO / caption |
|---|------|-----------------------------|------------------------|--------------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:04 | Black → tap-open the app, land on the **dashboard** | **Still running your café on paper chits?** | *(hook — no VO needed)* |
| 2 | 0:04–0:06 | Logo / name card | **Cafe POS** | *(music beat)* |
| 3 | 0:06–0:17 | **Menu → tap 3–4 items → cart fills** (slow, deliberate taps) | **Take the order in seconds** | One tap per item. The cart totals itself. |
| 4 | 0:17–0:26 | Switch to **Kitchen display** — the order *appears* live, tap to mark preparing/ready | **Kitchen sees it instantly** | No chits. No shouting. It's already there. |
| 5 | 0:26–0:36 | **Bill screen → print bill OR tap UPI → payment success** | **Bill printed · UPI & GPay built in** | Print the bill, or take a UPI payment — no extra machine. |
| 6 | 0:36–0:46 | **Dashboard / reports** — today's sales, best-sellers, payment mix | **Know exactly what you earned today** | Sales, best-sellers and payment mix — tallied automatically. |
| 7 | 0:46–0:54 | (Bonus) **Customer QR menu** — scan/open, browse, order | **Customers order from their own phone** | No app for them to install. |
| 8 | 0:54–1:00 | Back to the app's hero screen → **CTA card** | **Free 14-day trial · 📞 your number** | Book a free 10-minute demo — link below. |

> **Total ≈ 60s.** If a scene feels long, trim it — 45s that moves beats 60s that drags.

---

## 2. Canva assembly (≈45 min)

1. **New design →** *Mobile Video* (1080×1920). *(For a YouTube/website version, also
   make a 1920×1080 Landscape copy.)*
2. **Upload** your screen recordings (and a logo PNG if you have one).
3. **Drop clips on the timeline** in the order above. Drag the ends to trim — aim for the
   beats in the table.
4. **Text overlays:** add each line from the table. Settings:
   - Font: **Fraunces** (headings) + **Inter** (captions) — same as your app/site.
   - Colour: **Gold `#E6B25A`** on the keywords, white for the rest.
   - Place **centre-low third** so it never covers the part of the screen you're tapping.
5. **Transitions:** *Dissolve*, 0.2s — between every clip. Nothing fancy.
6. **Music:** Canva audio → search *“upbeat corporate”* / *“lofi chill”*, **low volume**
   (you should still read the captions). Mute the original screen-recording audio.
7. **Captions:** add them as text (don't rely on auto-captions) — most people watch muted.
8. **CTA card (last 3s):** big gold **“Book a free demo”** + your phone number + WhatsApp.

### Make it feel premium (2 tiny tricks)
- Add a **slow zoom** (Canva → Animate → *Pan*) on the dashboard shot.
- Start and end on a **gold-outlined phone frame** — reads as “product,” not “screen recording.”

---

## 3. Export specs for Google Ads

- **Format:** MP4 (H.264) + AAC audio
- **Aspect:** 9:16 (1080×1920) for Reels/Shorts/in-feed · 16:9 (1920×1080) for YouTube in-stream
- **Length:** 60s works for **skippable in-stream** and **in-feed**
- **File size:** keep under 1 GB (Canva easily)
- **First 5 seconds must hook** — that's all most people see before skipping

> Canva: **Share → Download → MP4 Video**, highest quality.

## 4. Also cut two shorter versions (10 min extra, big payoff)

| Cut | Length | Use it for |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| **Bumper** | **6s** | Scenes 3→5→8 only, no VO, just overlay text + music. YouTube bumper ads. |
| **Short** | **15s** | Scenes 1→3→5→8. Instagram Reels / WhatsApp status. |

Reuse the same timeline — just delete the middle scenes and re-export.

## 5. Where to post it (free)

1. **YouTube** (upload the 16:9) → then you can *embed it on the landing page* instead of the MP4.
2. **Instagram Reels + WhatsApp Status** (the 15s) — café owners actually scroll these.
3. **Google Ads** → run the 9:16 as in-feed / the 16:9 as skippable in-stream, sending
   clicks to your landing page.
4. **Pinned on your WhatsApp Business** profile.

### Quick checklist before you hit publish
- [ ] Every scene shows the **real app moving**
- [ ] Text is readable in **< 2 seconds** per card
- [ ] Ends on a **clear CTA with your number**
- [ ] Music is **quiet**, captions are **burned in**
- [ ] Tested on a phone, **muted**, on mobile data
