# Channel playbook — marketing beyond ads

Google Ads (see [`google-ads-campaign.md`](google-ads-campaign.md)) is the
*paid* lever. This is the free/cheap map — where to point the assets you
already own. Pick from the top; every row is real, in priority order.

## Tier 1 — free, start this week (~1 hr/day)

| # | Channel | What to do | Why it fits you |
|---|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | **WhatsApp Status** | Post `demo-60s.mp4` as Status **daily**; save a demo-link quick reply in WhatsApp Business; reply <5 min | India's #1 business channel — every café owner in your contacts views Status free |
| 2 | **Instagram Reels** | Post the 9:16 as Reels from a `@cafepos`-style handle; **comment usefully on 20 café pages/day** in target cities | Café owners run their café's IG professionally — your handle appears in front of *their* followers |
| 3 | **Buyer directories** | Free listings: **IndiaMART, JustDial, SoftwareSuggest** (~30 min each) | Owners actively search these for POS systems — existing demand, not created demand |
| 4 | **Facebook groups** | 3–5 café/restaurant-owner groups: genuinely answer a billing/GST question, demo link in the post | SMB-owner groups in India are large and active; one good post > 20 spammy ones |
| 5 | **YouTube organic** | Both videos are going up for ads anyway — searchable title: *"Café billing software India — 60 sec demo"* | Demo-seeking searchers find you free |

## Tier 2 — compounding, free, slower

| # | Channel | What to do |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 6 | **Referrals, formalized** | ₹500–1,000 or 1 free month per **paying** referral. Partners who already talk to cafés: GST accountants, coffee roasters, café consultants, interior designers — finder's fee per result (you pay only on success) |
| 7 | **Counter card + one-pager** | Leave the printed one-pager at every café you visit; give trial cafés a small *"Billing by Cafe POS"* counter card — their counter sees owners and customers daily |
| 8 | **Content/SEO** | 4–6 articles on the site: *"GST billing for cafés"*, *"UPI QR for small café"*, *"stop chit-order chaos"* + Quora answers. Compounds into organic Google traffic in months |
| 9 | **LinkedIn founder posts** | "I built a POS for cafés" story + screenshots — Indian startup/SMB circles reshare this |

## Tier 3 — small money

| # | Channel | What to do |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 10 | **Meta (Instagram) ads** | Same 9:16 video, interest-targeted: restaurant/café interests + page admins. Split test: ₹150 Google + ₹150 Instagram, let day-14 numbers pick the winner |
| 11 | **Micro-sponsorships** | One ₹2–5k story/reel from a small café-advice IG/YouTube page their audience already trusts |

## Skip (honest list)

- **Bulk cold WhatsApp / DMs** — gets your number banned (WhatsApp business policy)
- **Bulk SMS** — TRAI DLT registration required; near-zero read rates anyway
- **Print/media ads** — no measurable reach for this audience
- **SEO as your only plan** — right direction, too slow to fund itself alone

## The 7-day plan (all ₹0)

```
Day 1:  WhatsApp Status #1 + set up WhatsApp Business quick reply
Day 2:  IndiaMART listing + first genuine Facebook-group answer
Day 3:  JustDial listing + 20 IG comments on café pages
Day 4:  SoftwareSuggest listing + YouTube titles/descriptions live
Day 5:  Reel #1 + Status #2 + ask 5 people for referrals
Day 6:  Reel #2 + another group + 20 IG comments
Day 7:  Review: which channel got replies? Double down next week.
```

Track the same 3 numbers as the sales loop (**README §4**): conversations →
trials → paying. A channel that produces no conversations in 2 weeks is
paused, regardless of how free it is.
